# Code of Conduct

Tuned TV follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
Tuned TV sigue el [Contributor Covenant, versión 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/es/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Our pledge

We want everyone to feel welcome here, whatever their experience, background, identity or language. We pledge to
act and interact in ways that keep this community open, friendly and harassment-free.

## What we expect

- Be kind, patient and helpful, especially with people taking their first steps with ADB or with git.
- Assume good faith; disagree with ideas, not with people.
- Accept constructive feedback, give it constructively, and own your mistakes.
- English and Spanish are both fine. Do not mock anyone's language skills.

## What we do not accept

- Insults, harassment, trolling, and personal or political attacks.
- Sexualised language or imagery.
- Publishing anyone's private information (addresses, e-mails, network details) without their permission.
- Anything else that would be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

Report a problem privately to the maintainers through
[GitHub's private reporting](https://github.com/ignaciopd3/tuned-tv/security/advisories/new), or by contacting a
maintainer through their GitHub profile. Every report is reviewed promptly and in confidence. The response is
proportionate: a reminder, a warning, a temporary or a permanent ban.
